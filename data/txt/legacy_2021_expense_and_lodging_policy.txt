# Archived Legacy 2021 Expense and Lodging Policy (Superseded)
Notice: SUPERSEDED BY 2024 POLICY - ARCHIVED REFERENCE ONLY. Policy Code: POL-EXP-2021-V3.

## 1. Archived Lodging Caps (2021)
- Standard nightly hotel reimbursement was capped at $175 USD per night excluding taxes.
- Tier 1 major metropolitan cities (New York, San Francisco, London, Tokyo) carried a maximum rate of $250 USD per night.
- Tier 2 designated cities (Chicago, Boston, Seattle, Paris) were capped at $185 USD per night.
- Any booking exceeding these 2021 rates required pre-approval from the corporate comptroller.

## 2. Archived Meal Allowances (2021)
- The daily meal per diem was capped at $50 USD per calendar day for domestic business travel.
- Alcohol was non-reimbursable under the 2021 expense schedule without executive VP exception.
- Personal laundry reimbursement was permitted only on business trips exceeding 10 consecutive nights.
