# Executive and C-Suite Flight Protocol Addendum

## 1. Flight Separation Protocol for Corporate Leadership
- Under no circumstances may the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) and Chief Financial Officer (CFO) travel on the same scheduled aircraft or private charter.
- No more than two members of the Executive Leadership Team may travel on the same flight itinerary, protecting organizational continuity against aviation casualties.

## 2. Executive Security Ground Transport
- C-suite executives traveling to high-risk international geopolitical regions are authorized for dedicated secure driver transport rather than commercial ride-shares.
