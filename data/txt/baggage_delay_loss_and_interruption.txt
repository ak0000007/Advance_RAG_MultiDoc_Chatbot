# Baggage Delay, Loss and Trip Interruption Protection

## 1. Baggage Delay and Essential Purchase Coverage
- Checked baggage delayed beyond 12 hours from arrival qualifies for reimbursement of essential clothing and toiletries up to $250.
- Completely lost, stolen, or damaged checked luggage is covered up to a maximum benefit of $1,500 per traveler upon presentation of an airline Property Irregularity Report (PIR).

## 2. Trip Interruption and Medical Bereavement
- Non-refundable itinerary cancellation costs caused by acute illness, hospitalization, or immediate family bereavement are reimbursable up to $10,000 per traveler.
- Voluntary trip cancellation due to general work fatigue or scheduling conflicts is strictly excluded from insurance claims.
