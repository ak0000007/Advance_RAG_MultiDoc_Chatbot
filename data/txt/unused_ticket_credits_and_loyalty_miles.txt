# Unused Airline Ticket Credits and Loyalty Status Guidelines

## 1. Ownership of Unused Flight Credits
- All non-refundable airline tickets purchased using corporate funds remain the sole property of the company upon flight cancellation.
- The Corporate Travel Department audits unused ticket credits quarterly; employees rebooking business travel must apply existing unused ticket credits before purchasing new airfare.
- Unused airline tickets generally expire 12 months from the original date of ticket purchase.

## 2. Personal Frequent Flyer Loyalty Miles
- Corporate travelers are permitted to accrue and retain airline frequent flyer miles and hotel loyalty points generated during business travel for personal use, provided preferred corporate booking vendors are utilized.
