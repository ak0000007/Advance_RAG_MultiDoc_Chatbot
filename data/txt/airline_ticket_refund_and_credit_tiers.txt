# Airline Ticket Refund Tiers and Cancellation Policy

## 1. Structured Cancellation Refund Tiers
- Cancellations submitted 7 calendar days or more prior to scheduled flight departure receive a 100% full refund or unrestricted corporate credit voucher.
- Cancellations submitted between 48 hours and 7 days prior to scheduled departure receive a 75% refund to the original corporate payment method.
- Cancellations submitted within 48 hours of scheduled departure qualify for a 50% refund.
- No-shows without prior notice receive 0% refund and immediately forfeit all ticket value and credit eligibility.

## 2. Refund Processing and Timelines
- All approved cash refunds are processed within 5 to 10 business days following submission of the cancellation request.
- Flight credits issued in lieu of refunds remain valid for booking for 12 months from the date of original issuance.
