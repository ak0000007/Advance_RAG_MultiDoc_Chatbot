# Corporate Fleet Motor Pool Operations Policy
Notice: Operating rules for company-owned motor pool vehicles dispatched from headquarters. Directive Code: FLEET-DIR-2023-A.

## 1. Motor Pool Vehicle Checkout Rules
- Internal fleet vehicles may be checked out for a maximum duration of 48 continuous hours without Facilities Director sign-off.
- Commercial rental agencies (Hertz, Enterprise) are governed separately under the travel policy and must not use motor pool checkout forms.
- Drivers must possess a valid state driver's license and complete the defensive driver verification module.

## 2. Fueling and Return Inspection
- All motor pool vehicles must be refueled at headquarters corporate pump #4 before keys are returned to dispatch lockbox.
- Failure to refuel fleet vehicles incurs a $35 administrative department recharge fee.
