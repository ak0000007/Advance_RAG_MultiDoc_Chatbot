# Archived 2021 Executive Charter Aviation Memo (Sunset)
Notice: SUNSET ARCHIVE - 2021 Executive Aviation Memo. Reference Code: EXEC-MEMO-2021-08.

## 1. Executive Charter Provisions (2021 Temporary)
- Chartered private air transit was temporarily authorized for corporate executive committee members during facility disruptions.
- Authorization required dual signatures from the Chief Executive Officer and Chairman of the Board.
- Charter aircraft agreements were permanently sunset on January 1, 2023; all executive travel has reverted to commercial airlines.
