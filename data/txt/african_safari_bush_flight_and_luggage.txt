# African Safari Expedition Bush Flight and Luggage Rules

## 1. Bush Flight Luggage Weight Limitations
- Regional bush aircraft transfers (Cessna Caravan and light turboprops) enforce an unnegotiable luggage weight limit of 15 kilograms (33 pounds) per passenger, inclusive of camera gear and hand luggage.
- Luggage must be packed exclusively in soft-sided duffel bags without rigid internal metal frames or wheels. Hard-shell rolling suitcases are strictly prohibited and will be rejected by pilots.

## 2. Safari Confirmation and Balance Schedules
- Luxury safari expeditions require a 20% non-refundable deposit upon reservation confirmation.
- Remaining balance must be remitted in full at least 60 calendar days prior to expedition departure.
- All safari participants must sign a comprehensive wildlife encounter and liability waiver prior to participating in open-vehicle game drives.
