# Business Meals and Incidental Expense Guidelines

## 1. Daily Meal Allowances (Per Diem)
- Domestic business travel meal per diem allowance is strictly capped at $75 per calendar day ($15 breakfast, $25 lunch, $35 dinner).
- International business travel meal per diem allowance is capped at $100 per day ($20 breakfast, $35 lunch, $45 dinner).
- Day trips with travel duration under 12 hours do not qualify for dinner per diem reimbursement.

## 2. Alcohol and Client Entertainment Restrictions
- Alcoholic beverages purchased during individual or solo employee meals are non-reimbursable personal costs.
- Business meals involving clients, prospects, or external vendors require itemized receipts listing all attendees and business topics discussed.
