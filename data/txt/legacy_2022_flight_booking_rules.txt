# Archived 2022 Flight Booking Guidelines (Superseded)
Notice: SUPERSEDED - ARCHIVED 2022 FLIGHT DIRECTIVE. Directive Code: FLT-REG-2022-09.

## 1. Archived Booking Advance Windows (2022)
- Domestic flights required a mandatory 10-day advance booking window prior to departure.
- International business flights required a 14-day advance booking window prior to departure.
- Exceptions required divisional Vice President written email authorization.

## 2. Archived Cabin Class Limitations (2022)
- Flights exceeding 8 continuous flight hours qualified for Business Class booking.
- Flights between 4 and 8 hours were restricted to standard Economy with paid extra legroom seats.
- Flights under 4 hours were strictly restricted to Economy class tickets.
