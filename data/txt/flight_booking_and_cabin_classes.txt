# Flight Booking and Cabin Class Regulations

## 1. Domestic and International Lead Times
- Domestic flights must be booked a minimum of 14 days in advance through the corporate travel portal.
- International business itineraries must be booked at least 21 days prior to scheduled departure.
- Any booking requested within 7 calendar days of departure is classified as an expedited booking and requires written approval from a department Director.

## 2. Permitted Cabin Classes of Service
- Flights with scheduled airborne duration under 6 continuous hours must be booked in Economy or Premium Economy.
- Flights exceeding 6 continuous hours qualify for Business Class seating upon written approval from a corporate Vice President.
- First Class travel is strictly prohibited across all domestic and international routes, regardless of rank or flight duration.

## 3. Airline Ticket Exchanges and Modifications
- Voluntary flight changes must be processed through corporate travel agents; airline change fees are covered only when requested due to business schedule modifications.
- Same-day flight changes (standby) on authorized airlines are permitted if the fee does not exceed $75.
