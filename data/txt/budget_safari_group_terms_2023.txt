# Budget Safari Overland Group Tour Terms & Conditions
Notice: Terms applicable strictly to overland group camping and budget wildlife excursions. Terms Code: SAF-OVR-2023-B.

## 1. Budget Tour Deposits and Payment Deadlines
- Budget safari group excursions require a 10% non-refundable deposit upon confirmation.
- Remaining balance must be received 30 calendar days before departure date.
- Bookings made within 30 days of trip departure require full upfront payment.

## 2. Overland Bush Flight Luggage Parameters
- Luggage weight allowance on budget overland safari bush flights is restricted to 20 kilograms (44 pounds) total.
- Soft-sided duffel bags are mandatory; no hard-shell framed suitcases are allowed on regional airstrips.

## 3. Cancellation Schedule
- Cancellations submitted 60 or more days prior to departure receive an 80% refund of payments beyond the deposit.
- Cancellations between 30 and 59 days receive a 50% refund.
- Cancellations under 30 days prior to departure forfeit 100% of the tour fee.
