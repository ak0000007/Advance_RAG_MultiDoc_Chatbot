# Corporate Travel Policy

## 1. Booking Timelines
- All domestic business travel must be booked at least 14 days in advance of departure.
- All international flights must be booked at least 21 days in advance of departure.
- Last-minute flight bookings (within 7 days) require written approval from a department Director.

## 2. Flight Class of Service
- Flights with scheduled flight duration under 6 hours must be booked in Economy or Premium Economy.
- Flights exceeding 6 continuous hours of flight time qualify for Business Class booking with Vice President approval.
- First Class travel is strictly prohibited on company expense across all routes.

## 3. Cancellation and Refund Policy
- Cancellations submitted 7 or more calendar days prior to scheduled departure receive a 100% full refund or credit voucher.
- Cancellations submitted between 48 hours and 7 days prior to departure receive a 75% refund.
- Cancellations submitted within 48 hours of departure are eligible for a 50% refund.
- No-shows without prior notice receive 0% refund and forfeit all travel credits.
