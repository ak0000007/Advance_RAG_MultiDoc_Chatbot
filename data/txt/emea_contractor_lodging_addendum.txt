# EMEA Contractor and Contingent Workforce Travel Addendum
Notice: Special provisions governing external independent contractors traveling in EMEA. Addendum Code: POL-EMEA-CWF-04.

## 1. Contractor Lodging Ceiling (EMEA)
- Independent contractors operating across EMEA territories are capped at 160 EUR per night inclusive of VAT.
- London hotel ceiling for independent contractors is capped at 180 GBP per night.
- Room service, mini-bar, and in-room movies are non-reimbursable contingent expenses.

## 2. Contractor Meal and Incidental Reimbursement
- Per diem flat allowances are not available to contingent contractors; all meal expenses must be itemized with VAT receipts.
- Maximum allowable meal expense is 45 EUR per travel day.
- Alcoholic beverages cannot be invoiced under contractor expense schedules under any circumstance.
