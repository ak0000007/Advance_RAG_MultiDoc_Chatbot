# Expense and Lodging Guidelines

## 1. Lodging and Hotel Caps
- Standard domestic lodging is capped at $250 per night excluding local taxes and mandatory resort fees.
- Designated high-cost metropolitan locations (New York City, San Francisco, London, Tokyo, Zurich) have an approved lodging cap of $350 per night.
- Any lodging reservation exceeding these standard caps requires prior manager approval before reservation confirmation.

## 2. Daily Meal Allowances (Per Diem)
- Domestic travel meal allowance is $75 per day ($15 breakfast, $25 lunch, $35 dinner).
- International travel meal allowance is $100 per day ($20 breakfast, $35 lunch, $45 dinner).
- Alcohol purchases during solo meals are non-reimbursable. Client entertainment dinner expenses require itemized receipts regardless of cost.

## 3. Ground Transportation
- Ride-share services (Uber, Lyft) should utilize Standard or Comfort tiers; Black or Luxury rides will not be reimbursed.
- Rental cars must be reserved in the Compact or Mid-Size vehicle category through authorized corporate portals.
- Receipts are mandatory for all ground transport expenses exceeding $25.
