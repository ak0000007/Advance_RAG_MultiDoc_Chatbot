# Corporate Group Retreats and Hotel Contract Regulations

## 1. Group Bookings and Attrition Clauses
- Any corporate travel reservation involving 10 or more sleeping rooms is classified as a Group Booking and must be negotiated by the Corporate Events Department.
- Room block contracts must not agree to hotel attrition clauses exceeding 15% without legal review and Vice President signoff.
- Banquet food and beverage minimum expenditures must not exceed $150 per attendee per day for corporate offsites.

## 2. Cancellation of Group Itineraries
- Hotel room blocks canceled within 30 days of the event date are subject to 100% room revenue damages as defined in the master service agreement.
