# APAC Regional Travel Guidelines Addendum
Notice: Regional addendum for employees based in or traveling within Asia-Pacific markets. Addendum Code: POL-APAC-2024-R1.

## 1. Regional Lodging Maximums (APAC)
- Singapore lodging is capped at 320 SGD per room night excluding service charge and GST.
- Tokyo lodging is capped at 35,000 JPY per room night.
- Sydney and Melbourne lodging is capped at 290 AUD per room night.
- All other APAC regional business centers are capped at 210 USD equivalent per night.

## 2. Regional Daily Meal Allowances (APAC)
- Daily meal allowance for Singapore is 120 SGD per calendar day.
- Daily meal allowance for Tokyo is 14,000 JPY per calendar day.
- Daily meal allowance for Sydney is 110 AUD per calendar day.
- Incidentals per diem across APAC destinations is fixed at 25 USD equivalent per day.
