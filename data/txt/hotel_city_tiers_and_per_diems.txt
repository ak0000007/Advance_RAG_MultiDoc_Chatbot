# Hotel City Tiers and Lodging Caps

## 1. Metropolitan Lodging Cap Classification
- Standard Domestic Lodging (Tier 2 Markets): Capped at $250 per night excluding local taxes and mandatory hotel facility fees.
- High-Cost Metropolitan Destinations (Tier 1 Markets): Defined as New York City, San Francisco, London, Tokyo, and Zurich. Approved nightly lodging cap is $350 per night.
- Small Regional Markets (Tier 3): Maximum approved lodging cap is $180 per night.
- Any lodging reservation exceeding these defined caps requires prior written justification and manager authorization.

## 2. Ineligible Lodging Expenses
- Personal in-room entertainment (pay-per-view movies), minibar snack purchases, and hotel spa services are strictly non-reimbursable personal expenses.
- Resort fees and mandatory destination charges will be reimbursed only when booked through the preferred corporate lodging program.
