# Medical Immunization and Emergency Evacuation Guidelines

## 1. Required Immunizations and Health Certificates
- Proof of Yellow Fever vaccination recorded in an official International Certificate of Vaccination (ICVP) is mandatory for entry into East African travel destinations (Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda).
- Anti-malaria oral prophylaxis (such as Malarone or Doxycycline) is strongly recommended for all safari travel participants.

## 2. Emergency Medical Evacuation Coverage
- Emergency medical transport and air ambulance evacuation are covered up to a maximum policy limit of $500,000 per incident for all premium tier corporate travel bookings.
- Evacuations must be pre-authorized and coordinated directly through the 24/7 Global SOS Assistance hotline to qualify for direct billing and full indemnification.
