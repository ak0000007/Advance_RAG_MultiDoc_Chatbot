# Archived 2020 Emergency Travel Voucher and Pandemic Cancellation Policy (Expired)
Notice: HISTORICAL ARCHIVE ONLY - EXPIRED AS OF DECEMBER 31, 2023. Policy Code: COVID-VOUCHER-2020.

## 1. Emergency Voucher Validity Rules
- Travel credit vouchers issued between March 1, 2020 and December 31, 2021 remained valid for 24 months from issuance date.
- All outstanding emergency pandemic vouchers permanently expired on December 31, 2023 and cannot be re-issued.
- Direct cash refunds were suspended during the declaration period in favor of non-transferable travel credits.

## 2. Force Majeure Involuntary Disruptions
- Flights grounded by government border restrictions triggered automatic issuance of 100% credit shells with airline partners.
