# Travel Insurance and Medical Protection Guidelines

## 1. Emergency Medical Evacuation
- Emergency medical evacuation is covered up to a maximum policy limit of $500,000 per incident for all premium tier corporate bookings.
- Medical evacuations must be coordinated through the 24/7 Global Assistance hotline to qualify for direct billing.

## 2. Trip Interruption and Cancellation Protection
- Trip interruption coverage reimburses non-refundable expenses up to $10,000 in cases of certified acute medical illness, personal injury, or immediate family bereavement.
- Routine travel fatigue or voluntary itinerary abandonment is excluded from insurance reimbursement.

## 3. Delayed and Lost Luggage Compensation
- Checked baggage delayed beyond 12 hours qualifies for essential emergency clothing and toiletry reimbursement up to $250.
- Completely lost or unrecoverable luggage is covered up to $1,500 per traveler upon submission of property irregularity report (PIR) from the carrier.
