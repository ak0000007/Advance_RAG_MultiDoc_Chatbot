# Emergency Crisis Evacuation and Security Protocols

## 1. Natural Disaster and Civil Unrest Response
- In the event of armed conflict, civil unrest, or catastrophic natural disasters occurring in an employee travel territory, the Corporate Crisis Management Team initiates an immediate repatriation and extraction protocol.
- Employees stranded in crisis zones are authorized for an emergency spending limit override up to $5,000 without prior written approval, provided expense receipts are submitted within 30 days of safe return.

## 2. Emergency Global Hotline Contact
- All international travelers are automatically registered in the enterprise SOS tracking portal; emergency assistance is reachable 24 hours a day via the dedicated SOS dispatch hotline.
