# Car Rental and Ground Transportation Regulations

## 1. Authorized Vehicle Rental Categories
- All corporate vehicle rentals must be booked within the Compact, Economy, or Mid-Size car categories.
- Full-size sedans, premium luxury vehicles, and large SUVs are strictly non-reimbursable unless transporting 4 or more business travelers together.
- Collision Damage Waiver (CDW) and Loss Damage Waiver (LDW) must be declined if renting domestically using authorized corporate charge cards that provide primary insurance.

## 2. Ride-Sharing and Taxi Guidelines
- Ride-share services (Uber, Lyft) must be reserved using standard Economy or Comfort tiers.
- Premium luxury ride-sharing options (Uber Black, Lyft Lux, private limousines) are strictly non-reimbursable.
- Itemized receipts are mandatory for all ground transport expenses exceeding $25.
