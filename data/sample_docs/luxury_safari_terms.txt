# Luxury Safari Expedition Terms & Conditions

## 1. Booking Deposits and Payment Schedules
- All luxury safari expeditions require a 20% non-refundable deposit at the time of reservation confirmation.
- Final remaining balance payment is due exactly 60 calendar days prior to expedition departure.
- Reservations made within 60 days of scheduled departure require 100% full payment at initial booking.

## 2. Bush Flight Baggage Restrictions
- Due to strict weight and cargo limitations on regional bush aircraft, all travelers are restricted to a maximum baggage allowance of 15 kilograms (33 pounds) per person.
- All luggage must be soft-sided duffel bags without rigid internal or external metal frames. Hard-shell suitcases are strictly prohibited on bush flights.

## 3. Health, Immunization, and Safety Requirements
- Proof of Yellow Fever vaccination is mandatory for entry into East African safari territories.
- Prophylactic anti-malaria medication is strongly recommended for all safari expedition participants.
- Guests must sign a wildlife encounters waiver prior to participating in open-vehicle game drives.
